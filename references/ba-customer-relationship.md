## What each guide covers (paraphrased summary — verify specifics against the live guide)

**Advanced Pricing (eoapg).** Extends base pricing (in Sales Order Management and Procurement) with adjustment schedules — a schedule groups and sequences multiple adjustment definitions, each of which sets a pricing hierarchy search order, controls (print on invoice? rebate/billback vs. accrual? level-break calculation? can it be overridden at runtime?), and a GL offset via AAIs. Adjustments can apply at line, basket (a group of items via a Basket Reprice Group code), or order level; basket-level adjustments on a purchase order calculate the discount off the combined quantity/weight/amount across all qualifying lines, not each line individually. Price groups let you define adjustments once for a customer/item group rather than per customer/item; a Pricing Workbench and Price Matrix give a consolidated view/edit surface across many pricing scenarios at once. Also supports free-goods catalogs, volume incentive/rebate tracking, mandatory adjustments (the system errors if a mandatory adjustment on the schedule wasn't actually applied to the order), and — via Blend Management — contract-based commodity pricing (e.g. paying growers based on quality-test results). Feeds AP (supplier rebate/ship-and-debit accrual) and AR (customer rebate/adjustment tracking, optionally printed on invoices).

**CRM Fundamentals / CRM Foundation (eoacf).** The shared setup every other CRM module (Sales, Support/Case Management, Service Management) requires first. Provides: CRM constants (process, sales team, sales order, "gold bars," calendar categories) that control how CRM information is processed; and entry/maintenance of employees, customers, contacts, and partners — all of which reuse the base Address Book (P01012) rather than duplicating data (partners typically use address-book search type "PA"). Also covers item/catalog setup for use in sales, service, and support processes, plus email/mail-merge and call-plan tools for service/support staff. Supports the "lead to cash" and "request to resolve" business processes that Sales and Support build on top of.

**CRM for Sales (eoasf).** Sales-force automation on top of CRM Foundation: qualification scripts to vet whether a lead/opportunity is worth pursuing, sales methodologies to standardize how a sale is worked, lead/opportunity/customer/contact tracking, quotes and sales orders generated directly from an opportunity, and pipeline analysis by employee, territory, or customer.

**CRM for Support / Case Management (eoamr).** Gives a single point of contact for capturing and resolving customer issues (cases), with configurable rules to auto-assign and escalate cases to the right provider group, and notification/distribution-list setup for assignment and escalation alerts. Ties into Failure Analysis (associating failures/analyses/resolutions with cases and, if licensed, service work orders) and Solution Advisor. Billing for cases follows the same mechanism as Service/Support Management generally: accumulate billable cases (Case Workfile Generation, R17675, reading eligible cases into F4812) → generate/print invoices → post to AR and GL — the same billing pipeline is used whether billing for contracts, service work orders, or cases.

**Service Management (eoasm).** Manages the post-sale service lifecycle: service/supplier contracts, equipment repair scheduling, warranty and supplier-recovery claims, and linking failures/analyses/resolutions to cases and work orders. Heavily integrated — reads/writes Work Orders, uses Service Billing to invoice contracts/work orders/cases, Advanced Pricing for contract/work-order/case pricing, Inventory Management for parts issued against a work order, Capital Asset Management/Resource Assignments/Condition-Based Maintenance/Equipment Cost Analysis for equipment-side data, and Sales Order Management to create installed-equipment records when new equipment is sold. Setup includes service/warranty constants, UDCs, next numbers, product family/model combinations, customer and service-provider records, and AAIs.

**Customer Self Service, In-Memory Sales Advisor** — not deep-dived in this pass; `web_search` the guide title for specifics (Customer Self Service = external customer-facing portal for service/order inquiries; In-Memory Sales Advisor = real-time sales analytics dashboard, same "In-Memory Advisor" family as the Planning Advisor in topic `ba-supply-chain-manufacturing`).
