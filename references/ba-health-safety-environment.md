## What each guide covers (paraphrased summary — verify specifics against the live guide)

**Health and Safety Incident Management (eoasi).** Records and manages any occupational event — not just employee injury/illness, but also security incidents (theft, assault), motor vehicle damage, property/asset damage, environmental spills, fires/explosions, near-misses, and observed unsafe conditions. An incident record (entered via the Incident Master program, P54HS00) can be linked to existing EnterpriseOne data — personnel, equipment/assets, the related employee establishment, projects, employee competencies, and personnel safety hours — rather than re-entering that context. Built-in statutory reports cover OSHA 300 (Log), OSHA 301 (Injury/Illness), OSHA 300A (Summary), and the BLS Survey of Occupational Injuries and Illnesses (Form BLS-9300N06); One View Reporting adds ad hoc analysis (who/what/where/when and safety-rate metrics). Notifications (email or Work Center) alert relevant people when an incident is entered, and (as of a 9.2 Update) user-defined incident data lets you capture org-specific fields beyond the base incident record, plus a task-tickler reminder for follow-up actions. Setup covers UDCs, category codes, safety-hours tracking, email distribution groups, next numbers, and establishments (the org units incidents are reported against — relevant to OSHA recordkeeping requirements). Integrates with Address Book, General Accounting, HCM/HR/Payroll (personnel and cost data), Capital Asset Management (equipment involved in an incident), Job Cost (project-related incidents), and Inventory Management (a spilled/released product or substance can be tied to its Item Master record).

**Environmental Accounting and Reporting (EAR).** Tracks greenhouse-gas (GHG) emissions and other environmental metrics (e.g. recycled printer cartridges, water pumped, passenger travel/fuel usage) against voluntary or legislated reduction targets. Setup spans several applications: activate the EAR system, set up environmental organizations (aggregate facility, facility, or other org types via UDC 79A/OT) with a size classification (Large/Medium/Small, UDC 79A/FS), and complete prerequisite setup in other modules the EAR data draws from. The Environmental Ledger stores emissions in kilograms internally but lets you choose a display unit of measure (e.g. tons) for the prebuilt Oracle BI dashboards. Company mailing-address setup for environmental organizations reuses the standard Address Book Revisions program (P01012).
